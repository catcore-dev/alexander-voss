Stories told in light.

Nina Kovacs is a Budapest-born director and photographer. She shoots fashion editorials, documentary portraits, and the occasional music video. Her work appears in Vogue, i-D, and the Venice Biennale.

Photography is patience. The rest is just gear.

Born in Budapest, 1987. Studied film at FAMU in Prague before moving to Paris in 2012. Shot her first Vogue editorial at 26 and never looked back.

Nina works exclusively on film for editorial work and reserves digital for commercial campaigns. She describes her style as "documentary with intention" — images that feel found rather than constructed.

Based between Paris and Budapest. Available for commissioned work worldwide.
