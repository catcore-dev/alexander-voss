Born in Vienna. Working everywhere.

Elias studied philosophy before picking up a camera. That sequence still matters. He approaches each commission as a question rather than a task — what is this image trying to become, and what does it need from us to get there.

His work has been published in Vogue, Harper's Bazaar, GQ, and The New York Times. He has shot campaigns for Hermès, Prada, and Maison Margiela, though he prefers independent projects and gallery commissions.

Currently based between Paris and Vienna, available for commissioned work worldwide.
